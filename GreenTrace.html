About GreenTrace

GreenTrace is a Multi-Product Life Cycle Analysis and Environmental Dashboard built as a BCA Internship Project. It visualizes the hidden environmental costs of 10 everyday consumer products across five life cycle stages, measuring CO² emissions, water usage, energy consumption, and waste generation for each product.

🎯
Project Objective
Build an interactive tool specifically for Indian college students to understand the environmental footprint of products they use daily, and guide them toward local eco-friendly alternatives.
🔬
Methodology
Data collected from environmental research reports, LCA studies, academic sources, and a Google Forms survey. Each product scored 0–100 based on aggregated environmental impact across all five life cycle stages.
👥
Stakeholders
Indian college students provided usage data specific to their daily lives. Faculty mentor guided methodology. Community members from Bengaluru contributed survey responses. 42 people tested the GreenTrace prototype across two survey rounds.
📊
Key Findings
Mobile phones have the highest impact score (95/100). 52% of Indian student respondents were unaware of life cycle impacts. 81% were interested in local eco-friendly alternatives. Single-use plastic packaging is the biggest waste contributor.

📐 Impact Scoring System
LevelScore RangeDescription
High Impact70 – 100Severe environmental damage across multiple stages. Alternatives strongly recommended.
Medium Impact50 – 69Moderate impact. Switching to alternatives provides meaningful benefit.
Low Impact35 – 49Below-average impact. Still has room for improvement, particularly at disposal.
Very Low0 – 34Minimal environmental impact. Good recyclability and low emissions across most stages.
