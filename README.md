# 🌱 GreenTrace – Indian College Student Carbon Footprint Tracker

[![GreenTrace Visual](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Project-GreenTrace-2ea44f?style=for-the-badge)](https://splendid-fudge-521fc5.netlify.app/)

**GreenTrace** is a sustainability-focused web project designed to help Indian college students understand the environmental impact of everyday products and make more informed, eco-friendly choices.

🔗 **Live Project:** [GreenTrace Visual](https://splendid-fudge-521fc5.netlify.app/)

---

## 🌍 About the Project

College students use many everyday products, but it can be difficult to understand their environmental impact.

GreenTrace provides a simple visual way to explore the environmental impact of everyday products through key sustainability indicators such as:

* 🌫️ CO₂ Emissions
* 💧 Water Usage
* ⚡ Energy Consumption
* 🗑️ Waste Generation

The project focuses on making sustainability information easier to understand and encouraging users to think about the environmental impact of their everyday choices.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of GreenTrace are to:

* Increase awareness about product-level environmental impact
* Present sustainability information in a simple and visual format
* Help students compare everyday products
* Encourage more environmentally responsible choices
* Make sustainability data accessible to a college-student audience

---

## 📊 Key Features

### 🌱 Environmental Impact Dashboard

Provides visual information about the environmental impact of everyday products.

### 📦 Multi-Product Analysis

The project analyzes **20+ everyday Indian products** across multiple environmental indicators.

### 🌍 Four Sustainability Indicators

Products are evaluated using:

| Indicator         | What it Represents                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 🌫️ CO₂ Emissions | Carbon emissions associated with the product    |
| 💧 Water Usage    | Water required throughout the product lifecycle |
| ⚡ Energy          | Energy consumption associated with the product  |
| 🗑️ Waste         | Waste generated during the product lifecycle    |

### 🔄 "Should I Switch?" Feature

The project includes a **"Should I Switch?"** feature that helps users think about alternative product choices based on environmental impact.

### 📈 Visual Data Presentation

Environmental information is presented through an easy-to-understand visual dashboard rather than complex datasets.

---

## 📋 Survey & Data Collection

As part of the project, a survey was conducted to understand students' awareness and purchasing behavior related to environmentally friendly products.

### Survey Highlights

* **55 respondents** participated in the survey.
* **78%** were aware that everyday products can affect the environment.
* Only **27%** reported always checking whether packaging is environmentally friendly.
* **69%** were aware of product life cycles.
* Product **quality** was selected more often than eco-friendly packaging when considering purchasing decisions.

These findings helped identify the gap between **environmental awareness and everyday purchasing behavior**.

## 💡 Problem Statement

Indian college students regularly use plastic and single-use products but may not have simple, accessible information about the environmental consequences of those products.

GreenTrace addresses this gap by presenting product-level environmental information in a simple and visual format.
